Temponome makes your Tapo and Govee lights pulse and change color with your music.

INSTALL
1. Drag Temponome onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder, right-click Temponome and choose Open, then Open again.
   (macOS asks this once because the app isn't signed with an Apple developer account.)
3. If macOS asks to use the microphone or to find devices on your local network, click Allow.

SOUND FROM THIS MAC
Install BlackHole 2ch (free) from https://existential.audio/blackhole/ and choose it in
Temponome > Settings > Sound. Or choose the Mac's microphone to listen to a speaker in the room.

UPDATING
Drag the new Temponome onto Applications and choose Replace. Your lights, colors and
timing are kept (they live in ~/Library/Application Support/Temponome/).

Don't run the old "Start Tapo Music" launcher at the same time as the app.
