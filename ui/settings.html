Measure your lights' delay

Uses the Mac's camera to see how long each light takes to light up, so flashes land on the beat. Dim the room, turn screen brightness up and point the camera at the light (or a wall it lights). About 10 seconds plus 20 per light. The video stays on this Mac.

Aim so the light is in the picture, keep the Mac still, then press Begin. The screen will flash black and white for a few seconds.
